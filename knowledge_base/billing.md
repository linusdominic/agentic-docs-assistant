# Plans and billing

## Plans

Nimbus Notes has three plans.

The Free plan includes up to 500 notes, sync across two devices, and attachments up to 10 MB each.

The Plus plan costs $6 per month or $60 per year. It includes unlimited notes, sync across unlimited devices, attachments up to 200 MB each, version history for 90 days, and offline access on mobile.

The Team plan costs $10 per member per month. It includes everything in Plus, shared notebooks with roles, single sign-on, and an admin console. Team plans need at least three members.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Team plans billed yearly can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms. Prices exclude sales tax and VAT, which are added at checkout based on your billing country.

## Refunds

You can request a refund within 30 days of your first payment on a monthly or yearly plan. Renewals of yearly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the renewal date. Renewals of monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid month. To request a refund, open Settings, choose Billing, and select Request refund. Refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Changing or cancelling a plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the prorated difference. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. If you downgrade to Free with more than 500 notes, nothing is deleted: your notes become read-only until you are under the limit or upgrade again.

## Student discount

Students and teachers with a valid academic email address get 50% off the Plus plan. Apply from Settings, Billing, Education discount. The discount is re-verified once a year.
